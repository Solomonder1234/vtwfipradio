About EAS Relay Network (ERN / VTWF)

The EAS Relay Network (ERN / VTWF) is a dedicated 24/7 meteorological telemetry, civil emergency alert relay, and live broadcast service serving the greater New York City metropolitan area and the national EAS community.

🚨

Emergency Alert System

Integrated with hardware DASDEC encoders/decoders and the GWES EAS Network. Relays live SAME/EAS warning bursts (EAN, TOR, SVR, FFW) with automated failover routing.

📡

Meteorological Ingestion

Pulls multi-office NWS data (Upton/OKX & Mount Holly/PHI), live NEXRAD Doppler radar loops, NOAA SPC convective outlooks, and Mesoanalysis sounding diagnostics.

🌊

Aviation & Marine Array

Monitors NYC metro airport METAR observations (JFK, LGA, EWR, TEB, FRG, ISP) with FAA flight categories and offshore NDBC ocean buoys (44065, 44017, 44025).
